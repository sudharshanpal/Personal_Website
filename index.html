AI-Powered Emerging Risk Intelligence Engine

The question was how to turn geopolitical and economic signals into something useful for reinsurance. I desgined an Azure data pipeline that ingests, normalizes, and classifies external risk data, then hooked up an Azure OpenAI engine to classify severity and assess mortality and longevity impacts.
